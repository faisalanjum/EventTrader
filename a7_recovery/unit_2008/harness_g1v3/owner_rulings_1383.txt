OWNER RULINGS — APPLY AS ONE GENERAL RULE SET, NEVER AS PER-EXAMPLE SPECIAL CASES:

1. Extract every distinct fact supported by a located quote. `raw_label_or_claim` is only a locator hint and cannot suppress another fact present in that quote.
2. Directly governing source text such as the row's header, definition or framing may identify its meaning. A neighbouring independent fact may never donate a value, scale or second fact. The quote must itself carry its scale. When source gives only fiscal shorthand, emit that shorthand and leave exact calendar-date resolution to the existing shared fiscal resolver.
3. A lawful `unknown`, a lawful source synonym, a code-normalizable off-menu spelling, or a Rule 1.4 abstention is a complete answer, not an open blocker. Preserve genuine rule uncertainty in the ambiguity exhibit. `open_issues` is only for uncertainty that still changes the final answer after these rulings.
4. One row represents one cause/name. Split independent facts and separate measurement bases. A shared level is emitted once on a compatible fact; another fact carries only its own change. Never allocate one combined amount among causes without source allocation. If no lawful aggregate representation exists, abstain.
5. Driver names contain no numbers, periods, motion words or glue words. A meaningful standard phrase may remain. A population qualifier belongs in the name; a company-owned group that scopes another metric is a slice. Measurement qualifiers and number modifiers belong in measurement. If the source does not expand a per-X acronym and the verified vocabulary cannot settle it, abstain rather than expand from outside knowledge or keep a bare acronym.
6. If two menu tokens are semantically indistinguishable and the source cannot choose, do not guess. Emit the source-grounded off-menu `kind:value`; existing code owns spelling normalization and restoration.
7. Direct source comparison can populate a baseline. Bare dated growth follows the existing OD-11 YoY rule. An unanchored basis-point change does not itself prove `prior_year`. Numberless growth retains the source-stated basis unit. A cumulative 24-week period is YTD, not `half`, unless the source calls it half.
8. `introduced` and `reaffirmed` require explicit source language. `keeps/remains on track` is explicit reaffirmation. A first bare guide is `unknown`; `not productive to update` is not reaffirmation.
9. Safe `unknown` surprise favourability is final. Company expectations or prior guidance route to `previous_guidance`, not consensus. A metric home and its surprise twin are distinct facts and need no dedupe group.
10. Emit a same-event semantic fact once even when later text repeats it with another quote or locator. Keep the first source-order canonical occurrence and record the later proposal as omitted/reconciled. The mechanical gate may compare exact normalized semantic records while ignoring only evidence locator and review metadata; code must not judge near-duplicate meaning.
11. An external facility or location that is not the reporting company's measured geography is not a geography slice. If source-grounded and necessary, keep that meaning in the name.
12. Do not force an open-ended delta into `change_value` or misuse `level_value`. If the contract cannot represent it lawfully, abstain and preserve the reason in the exhibit.
