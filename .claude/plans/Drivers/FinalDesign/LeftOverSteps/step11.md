# Step 11 — Prove Shadow Operation and One Bounded Graph Write

## Goal

Prove that the finished V2 system can operate against the real graph, then make
one small, separately approved Fiscal write and account for every graph change.

```text
frozen real events
→ complete production path with writes off
→ exact shadow plans and outcomes
→ isolated transaction and recovery proof
→ fresh owner approval
→ required graph setup only
→ one bounded Fiscal write
→ exact planned-versus-written reconciliation
→ Step 9B read-only proof
```

This is the first step that may change the Driver graph. It does not authorize a
full Fiscal harvest, another channel, old-Guidance deletion, native XBRL, or an
unbounded production schedule.

## Required starting state

Do not begin graph activation until:

* Steps 0–8, 9A, and 10 are complete, committed, pushed, and independently
  verified;
* V2 is the only reachable public and internal contract;
* the shared reader, admission system, validator, fusion owner, writer planner,
  read layer, catalog, certified Fiscal command, and running layer exist;
* the production catalog passed its gauntlet and fitness gate;
* the required model-free merge detectors, ATTACH audit, recovery path, and
  park/outage controls exist;
* every enabled model role is frozen to Sonnet 5 at high effort;
* the V2 route still refuses graph writes by default;
* Step 9B is visibly open only because lawful real Driver anchors do not yet
  exist;
* the exact candidate, environment, dependencies, configuration, catalog,
  model pins, source population, and test inventory are frozen in an isolated
  clean worktree;
* no unresolved owner decision affects the bounded pilot.

Return any missing behavior to its owning earlier step. Do not recreate it
here.

## Authority

Apply these owners in order:

1. `FINAL_DESIGN.md` for identity, birth, evidence, recovery, graph shape,
   reads, and safety law;
2. the live V2 `ChannelContract.md` and `15_CandidateFactPacket.md` for public
   events, internal facts, outcomes, retries, and channel acknowledgements;
3. `BUILD_AND_OPERATIONS.md` §§5, 7, 8.1, 10, and 11 for the writer transaction,
   preflight, shadow phase, S3 comparison, launch blockers, hazards, and
   recovery;
4. the frozen outputs and signed evidence from Steps 1–10;
5. current production code, tests, and fresh read-only graph results at the
   frozen candidate.

`STATUS_AND_HISTORY.md` records status only. This work order, comments, old
receipts, scratch files, and archived designs are leads, not behavior owners.

## Scope

Step 11 includes only:

* a fresh read-only census of the live graph and every premise used by setup or
  writing;
* the smallest transaction implementation needed by the existing Core writer;
* the exact three required uniqueness constraints and four required period
  sentinels;
* isolated setup, transaction, rollback, crash, lock, and idempotency proof;
* a complete no-write shadow run over a frozen real Fiscal population;
* the S3 synchronous-versus-asynchronous LINK comparison with CLAIM still off;
* the existing detector, audit, quarantine, recovery, and alert proofs;
* one explicitly bounded Fiscal graph-write pilot after fresh owner approval;
* exact before/plan/after reconciliation for every node, relationship,
  property, constraint, sentinel, outcome, audit, and lifecycle record;
* the read-only Step 9B gate immediately after lawful real anchors exist;
* reviewed commits and evidence for this exact candidate.

It excludes:

* another writer, transaction layer, object mapper, migration framework,
  schema registry, queue, scheduler, or recovery service;
* catalog bulk-sync or any factless Driver node;
* a semantic rule, regex, word list, threshold, exception, source matcher, or
  identity shortcut;
* CLAIM activation unless the exact S3 gate passes and the owner separately
  approves it;
* a full Fiscal harvest before Step 9B;
* any later channel, native-XBRL materializer, consumer cutover, old-Guidance
  change, or historical replay;
* a production graph mutation, deployment, commit, or push without its
  required exact approval;
* an unplanned or over-ceiling model call;
* unrelated cleanup, dependency upgrades, test moves, file restoration, or
  history rewriting.

## Non-negotiable rules

* Reuse the existing Core planner, writer, audit, single-writer lock, preflight,
  IDs, validators, period owner, and recovery owners. Add no parallel path.
* Dry-run and write mode must make the same reads and final plan. Write mode may
  differ only by executing that rechecked plan in one transaction.
* The write gate requires both the explicit call choice and the exact approved
  environment/configuration gate. Default remains no write.
* One source event is one non-retried transaction. Failure writes no partial
  Driver, fact, period, relationship, or audit success state.
* A new Driver and its first accepted fact are born together or not at all.
* Recheck source, company, Driver state, type, collisions, required family
  links, and write preconditions inside the transaction.
* Never execute a provisional out-of-transaction plan blindly.
* Driver and fact identities never change during recovery. Recovery may only
  apply the approved reversible states and must emit its immutable event.
* CLAIM remains off unless S3 earns it. A confirmed wrong synchronous link that
  the asynchronous arm avoided keeps it off.
* Every uncertainty or mismatch stops, parks, rolls back, or leaves the graph
  unchanged. Nothing is repaired by guesswork.
* TDD is required for every write-enabling behavior. Each negative test has a
  lawful control, and each high-risk guard has a mutation or deliberate-fault
  proof.
* Neo4j writes—including isolated setup tests, live constraints, sentinels, and
  the pilot—require explicit owner approval for the exact target and scope.

## Gate 11.0 — Freeze the candidate and denominator

Before editing or running anything:

1. Record commit, tree, staged state, dependency versions, configuration,
   model pins, catalog hashes, source manifests, and active feature flags.
2. Derive every reachable write-capable entry point, transaction call, setup
   command, preflight branch, write operation, failure branch, audit state,
   recovery action, and test from live code.
3. Derive the exact pilot-eligible Fiscal event population from the certified
   selector and Step 10 ledger. Do not hand-pick a successful event.
4. Freeze the owner-proposed bounded pilot selection before seeing its write
   result. The owner must approve the exact event count and selection rule.
5. Record every graph label, relationship, property, constraint, index, and
   sentinel the planned write may touch.
6. Account separately for existing Driver objects, old Guidance objects,
   source objects, and unrelated graph populations.
7. Map every denominator row to one owner, test/evidence requirement, and
   completion condition.

Gate 11.0 closes only with zero unexplained path, operation, side effect, or
eligible event.

## Gate 11.1 — Take a fresh read-only graph census

Using read-only transactions only, record the database identity, query text,
parameters, execution time, raw results, and hashes for:

* Driver, DriverUpdate, DriverPeriod, Driver relationship, and relevant index
  and constraint counts;
* the exact presence and property shape of the four period sentinels;
* source ownership cardinality and every enabled source type;
* ID, period, company, concept, member, dimension, and unit shapes the writer
  assumes;
* duplicate or conflicting IDs and every pre-existing object that could collide
  with the pilot;
* old Guidance nodes, relationships, constraints, and shared period objects;
* every graph category used by the detector, read, recovery, or Step 9B paths.

Historical counts may guide the queries but cannot replace this census. Stop on
any schema or population shape the frozen code does not understand.

## Gate 11.2 — Prove setup and the real transaction in isolation

Write failing tests first, then implement only the missing write surface in the
existing adapter/transaction owner.

The isolated proof must cover:

1. creating only the required uniqueness constraints for `Driver.name`,
   `DriverUpdate.id`, and `DriverPeriod.id`;
2. creating exactly the four contract-owned period sentinels with complete
   properties and null dates;
3. repeated setup converging without changing an existing lawful object;
4. conflicting constraint, sentinel, date, or property state failing closed;
5. the single-writer lock refusing a concurrent writer;
6. in-transaction source/company/Driver/family/collision rechecks;
7. lazy born-complete Driver plus first fact in one transaction;
8. existing-Driver fact creation, no-op rerun, allowed fill, collision sibling,
   and every existing planner outcome;
9. exact relationship and property creation—including Step 4 continuation
   links and claims with their duplicate, conflict, cycle, quarantine, and
   recovery guards—with no null-clobbering;
10. failure injected before and after each planned write boundary rolling back
    the whole event;
11. a leftover `prepared` audit remaining visibly unresolved rather than being
    assumed committed;
12. retrying the exact same lawful event converging, while a real source
    revision remains a distinct governed event;
13. audit state becoming `committed` only after transaction success;
14. graph reads after commit matching the final in-transaction plan exactly.

Use an isolated disposable database with a recorded empty or frozen starting
state. This gate does not authorize a live graph mutation.

## Gate 11.3 — Run the full no-write shadow

Run the frozen real Fiscal population through the production selector, source
locator, reader, admission system, validators, planner, operations ledger, and
reads with writes disabled.

Every model call uses Sonnet 5 at high effort under the frozen runbook and
needs no separate owner approval.

Account for:

* every selected, fetched, submitted, parsed, accepted, merged, parked,
  skipped, rejected, failed, retried, reopened, and acknowledged item;
* every proposed Driver, fact, period, relationship, link, recovery state, and
  graph setup change;
* every source-completeness, cursor, budget, canary, alert, and stop result;
* every difference between repeated frozen replay and live model output;
* exact before/after graph identity proving no mutation.

Replay all saved responses twice and require byte-identical requests, plans,
outcomes, audits, receipts, reads, and lifecycle state. Live model wording need
not be repeatable; its raw bytes and downstream outcome must be preserved and
accounted.

Any wrong accepted fact, wrong identity, unexplained planned write, missing
item, future-information leak, non-idempotent replay, silent retry, or graph
movement blocks activation.

## Gate 11.4 — Run S3 with CLAIM still off

Pre-register and hash-lock the sample, truth, graders, model roles, settings,
metrics, bars, and two forked starting states.

Run the same proposals in strict point-in-time order through:

* the synchronous CLAIM candidate arm, which logs the would-be link only; and
* the asynchronous LINK path.

Measure:

* confirmed wrong links in each arm;
* false refusals and safe splits;
* reuse and reconciliation recall;
* time to reconcile and duplicate half-life;
* head-meaning drift across sequential approvals against the frozen birth
  anchor;
* all rival, high-impact, cross-company, cross-industry, and homonym strata;
* detector, ATTACH-audit, planted-calibration, grader-disagreement, quarantine,
  recovery, and park-drain results.

Every identity grade must use the frozen raw evidence and the approved
independent Sonnet 5 high-effort review. A producer call never grades itself.

S3 can only make CLAIM eligible for a separate owner decision when synchronous
CLAIM has zero confirmed wrong links and all other frozen bars pass. It remains
off otherwise. The asynchronous path and recovery machinery still ship.

## Gate 11.6 — Provision only required graph structures

After approval:

1. recheck the candidate and live graph identity;
2. create only the three required uniqueness constraints and missing lawful

3. run preflight and require its exact ready result;
4. compare the graph with the approved setup plan;

## Gate 11.7 — Execute one bounded Fiscal write pilot

Immediately before execution, recheck approval, feature flags, writer lock,
candidate identity, source bytes, catalog, model pins, graph identity, and
preflight.

Run only the approved events. For each event:

1. preserve exact input bytes before interpretation;
2. run the normal production path once;
3. require the durable prepared audit before mutation;
4. execute the rechecked final plan in one transaction;
5. consume the final receipt in the normal operations ledger;
6. read back every expected node, relationship, property, and audit;
7. compare planned and actual results exactly;
8. prove all unrelated graph categories are unchanged;
9. stop the pilot on the first unexplained difference.

Never auto-retry a failed write. Apply only the frozen manual reconciliation or
recovery procedure.

## Gate 11.8 — Run Step 9B before any wider Fiscal write

After the pilot creates lawful real metric Driver anchors:

1. disable writes;
2. freeze the exact post-pilot graph identity;
3. run Step 9B's answer-blind 150-case tagged-filing gate read-only;
4. require all 150 cases accounted, zero observed wrong accepts, current Route-A
   regressions green, and no graph movement.

If fewer than 150 eligible anchors exist, Step 9B remains blocked. Do not widen
the pilot merely to manufacture a test population. The owner may approve a
separately justified bounded population increase only for production value,
not to game the gate.

No broader Fiscal harvest may begin until Step 9B passes and the owner approves
that separate scope.

## Gate 11.9 — Complete the initial controlled Fiscal rollout

After Step 9B passes, present a new finite rollout packet. The owner freezes the
exact companies, source range, event count, schedule, budget, stop limits, and
observation period before approving it.

Run in measured waves. For every wave:

* reconcile eligible sources, submitted events, raw items, outcomes, graph
  changes, reads, lifecycle records, cursors, costs, alerts, parks, retries, and
  recovery actions;
* require zero observed wrong accepted fact or identity and zero unexplained
  graph change;
* keep CLAIM and every unrelated feature at its separately approved state;
* stop on the first failed bar and do not start the next wave;
* obtain fresh approval before increasing the frozen scope.

The rollout is sufficient for Step 12 only when the owner-frozen initial scope
has completed its observation period with stable writes, reads, operations,
alerts, rollback, and recovery. It is not authority for another channel or an
unbounded historical harvest.

## Required proof

The exact candidate must pass:

* focused transaction, preflight, setup, writer, kernel, recovery, read,
  Fiscal, and operations tests;
* every affected regression and the full suite;
* 100% coverage of new or changed behavior branches and exception outcomes;
* required mutation tests for write gating, rechecks, rollback, audit state,
  identity, constraints, sentinels, receipt accounting, and recovery;
* process-crash tests at every durability boundary;
* concurrency and lock tests;
* deterministic replay and permutation tests;
* fresh read-only real-data and graph-shape evidence;
* isolated database before/after proofs;
* live shadow before/after proof;
* live pilot exact before/plan/after proof;
* Step 9B evidence after the pilot.

No skipped or missing relevant test identity is accepted without a named,
authority-backed reason.

## Reviewed publication sequence

Keep independently reviewable changes separate:

1. write-surface and setup implementation with isolated tests;
2. shadow/S3 proof code and signed evidence;
3. reviewed activation configuration, if any, with secrets excluded;
4. live setup and bounded-pilot receipts;
5. Step 9B evidence and status update.

Each code commit must be reviewed and published before it becomes the identity
for a live mutation. Graph actions are not hidden inside a code commit. Verify
local, staged, committed, origin, remote, and graph identities at each approved
boundary. Never force-push.

## Stop conditions

Stop if:

* any prerequisite or candidate identity is unknown;
* the graph census differs from an assumed schema or population;
* a required fixed value lacks a live owner;
* a write requires copied semantic logic or a second writer;
* setup would create anything beyond the approved constraints and sentinels;
* the shadow has a wrong accept, wrong identity, missing outcome, future leak,
  unexplained plan, or graph change;
* S3, a launch blocker, detector, recovery, gauntlet, or fitness gate is red or
  incomplete;
* an isolated fault leaves a partial graph change or false committed audit;
* a live mutation lacks exact fresh owner approval;
* the live graph moves between approval and execution;
* planned and actual pilot results differ;
* a credential, unrelated file, cursor, cache, source, old Guidance object, or
  unapproved feature changes;
* Step 9B observes a wrong accept or cannot form its lawful denominator;
* a required test, branch, mutation, receipt, or graph row is unaccounted.

## Completion condition

Step 11 is complete only when:

* the live graph premises are freshly measured and fully accounted;
* the existing Core writer has one proven transaction implementation and no
  parallel write path;
* setup, write, rollback, recovery, crash, lock, and idempotency behavior pass
  in isolation;
* the complete production path passes shadow with zero graph change and zero
  observed wrong accepted fact or identity;
* S3 is fully measured and CLAIM remains off unless it separately earned and
  received approval;
* the owner approved the exact live setup and bounded pilot immediately before
  mutation;
* only the required constraints, sentinels, and approved pilot data changed;
* every planned and actual graph change, item outcome, audit, receipt, and
  lifecycle state agrees exactly;
* rollback and recovery are proven and no partial state exists;
* Step 9B passes all 150 lawful cases read-only with zero observed wrong accepts
  and no graph movement;
* the separately approved initial Fiscal rollout completes in bounded measured
  waves with stable writes, reads, operations, alerts, rollback, and recovery;
* no later channel, consumer move, Guidance change, or native-XBRL activation
  occurred;
* exact reviewed code, evidence, local, remote, and graph identities are
  recorded.

The next step is Step 12: move consumers and retire old Guidance, record the
empty first-release Step 12B set while preserving its future-channel work
order, then build native-XBRL materialization last under its separate gates.
