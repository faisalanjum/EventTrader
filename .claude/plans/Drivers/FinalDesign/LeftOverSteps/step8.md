### Ranking and time

For the same company, exact series, and Eastern calendar day:

```text
8-K > transcript > 10-Q > 10-K > news
```

Then choose the later absolute timestamp and source ID. Across days, the latest
lawful fact is current and prior facts remain history. Amendments are new facts
at their own public time.

  ranked last;

### Rendering and policy behavior

Render in this order:

1. level point/range/floor/ceiling;
2. signed change;
3. comparison;
4. lawful guidance `value_text`;
5. truncated verbatim quote as display fallback only.

Use Driver name plus fact-scope suffix when a citation needs disambiguation.

decision is an action. Direction plus basis/percentage points without “to” is a

stored.

### Member-anchored display grouping

Group label drift only when, for one company and slice part:

* each label has at least one linked fact;
* all linked facts for that label agree on one exact stored axis/member pair;
* both labels resolve to that same pair.

The pair is the group key. The latest label may be displayed. Conflicts are
logged and stay split; linkless facts retain their own label. This changes only
display grouping—never fact identity or storage.

### Company-declared continuity

`ContinuationClaim` records.

The reconciled view must:

* follow old to new only within the same company and endpoint kind;
* require `declared_at < as_of` at every hop;
* suppress a quarantined hop regardless of when quarantine was discovered;
* stop at the terminal label;
* refuse fan-out, fan-in ambiguity, cycles, and mixed endpoint kinds;
* never cross Driver family/flavor boundaries;
* make no model call;
* never move, delete, re-key, or rewrite a fact.

Raw views never follow continuity. Build exactly one reconciled implementation;
the deferred unknown-axis grouping view stays absent.

### Guidance movement

`effective_driver_state` is read-derived and never written back.

* Preserve an explicitly stored raised, lowered, reaffirmed, or withdrawn state.
* For a bare numeric guide stored as unknown, compare only with the prior
  collapsed current value in the exact canonical guidance series.
* No prior value means introduced.
* Closed comparable midpoints derive raised, lowered, or reaffirmed.
* Open, mixed, numberless, cross-period, cross-slice, cross-measurement,
  cross-unit, or otherwise ambiguous comparison stays unknown.
* A real correction with no business-change signal stays unknown.
* Late facts re-derive the timeline on the next read.

No Driver-name rule, prose pattern, unit-family map, or history rewrite is
allowed.
