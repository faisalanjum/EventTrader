Never weaken expectations or silently regenerate baselines. Measured simplification must preserve all required behavior, failure/restart handling and coverage.
- Root owns strategy, priorities, design and independent acceptance. Use both Claude specialists for bounded, non-overlapping work; Test Agent keeps answers private and independent. Accept evidence after review, not green counts alone. Reassess after every milestone.
- **Outside this extraction scope:** document trees/hierarchy experiments, downstream wrappers/selection/chunking, driver creation, news/transcripts and live-ingestion hookup. The [separate tree lead](DocumentTreeHandover.md) may read versioned outputs without delaying this work or seeing private answers. Nmedia stays parked.
