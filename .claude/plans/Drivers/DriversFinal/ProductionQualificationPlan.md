- HTML/XML and nonreading picture connections remain in scope. OCR/PDF reading, downstream work, deployment, deletion and Neo4j writes remain excluded.

Report three separate measures: **work completed**; **document disposition** (verified/incomplete/failed/unstarted, with unknown reasons); **source fidelity** (fully reviewed/no required issue found, issue found, unread). An incomplete-document fraction is not a missing-content fraction.
