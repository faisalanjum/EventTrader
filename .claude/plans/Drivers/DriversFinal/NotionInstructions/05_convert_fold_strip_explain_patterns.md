**2. Replace the block "What the strip shows".**

Delete the bold line `What the strip shows` and its six bullets. In their place, outside the toggle and directly after it, put this heading and table.

Heading (same level as the other section headings): `Patterns in that document`

Table with a header row:

| What you see | Rows | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|
| A row that holds only a page number, such as `- 2 -` | 14, 23, 27, 33, 36, 46, 59, 64 | Where each printed page ends. |
| A tiny table with one filled cell, right above a big table | 24 above 25 and 26 · 28 above 29 · 30 above 31 · 34 above 35 | The tiny table reads as the big table's title, for example "CONSOLIDATED BALANCE SHEET". |
| A sentence right above a table | 41 above 42 · 51 above 52 · 54 above 55 | The sentence introduces that table, for example "The following table presents…". |
| A paragraph that starts with (1), (2), (3) or (4) | 38, 43, 54, 56 | Where each numbered note begins. |
| One title spread over two rows | 2 and 3 | A unit can be only part of something: "…Results for the First" then "Quarter of 2023". |
| One sentence broken by a page | 58, then 61 and 62 | Row 58 stops mid-sentence. It continues after the page number (59) and a repeated heading (60). |
| The kind "heading" cannot be trusted | 15 and 51 | "Capital Management" is a section title but came back as text. "The following table provides…" is a sentence but came back as a heading. |

In the Rows column, show the page number example and nothing else as code. Row numbers are plain text.
