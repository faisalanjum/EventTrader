# Guidance extraction: scoring contract

2026-11-15, America/Toronto (EST). Derived from the live `DRIVER_RULES_Categorized.md`; this adds no graph rules. Use this before labeling or grading. Legacy GuidanceUpdates and the draft 69-fact inventory are comparison material, not truth.

**Owner clarification, 2026-11-15:** previous Guidance/GuidanceUpdate records used the old structure; the current Driver rulebook governs this experiment's intended records. Verified rulebook SHA-256: `f9376dcb51f648d58fc1527ca3472fcce6f0ca1bb517969ccc250077cad78ff0`. Separate finding the source claim from representing it under the current rules. A legacy-schema difference is not automatically an extraction miss; legacy agreement does not prove a correct new record. Recheck the rulebook hash before freezing the reference or applying a strategy change.

1. **Eligible claim:** a specific financial/operating forecast, target, reaffirmation or withdrawal stated or clearly confirmed by management. A hypothetical illustration, historical result, analyst-only estimate, planned action or vague optimism is not automatically guidance (1.23, 1.26, 4.9). Preserve the question when it is needed to understand or prove management's answer.
2. **Unit of scoring:** one distinct forecast meaning, subject, scope and target period—not one number, endpoint, sentence or model question. A sentence can contain several forecasts. Repeated source targets are not automatically separate facts; identical text or numbers do not prove sameness (3.2, 5.2–5.9).
3. **Meaning and qualifiers:** the proposal must identify the measure, subject and applicable business scope, preserving every meaning-changing modifier. Raw source wording can be extracted before canonical Driver matching; neither an exact quote nor a plausible term proves the correct association. Period words must not become a final metric name, and measurement versions belong in tags (2.17–2.18, 3.24–3.27). Do not feed existing metric names into blind discovery (1.14).
4. **Amounts:** keep exactly the stated point, range, bound or meaningful numberless forecast. Exact rescaling is allowed; invented midpoints, endpoints, comparisons and corrected transcription are not. Numbers code already owns do not need another Locate copy. A current guidance growth/change amount is its value; `change` is an explicitly stated revision, with the source's change wording preserved (1.13, 3.48, 4.6). Apply the stated quantity's sign, not the mere direction of an expected stock effect.
5. **Units:** the actual quote must support unit, scale and growth basis; the only currency default is 9.1. A genuine source-unstated basis may be `unknown`; missing a basis that the source does establish is an extraction failure. Apply all of 3.33, including points/basis-points precedence and annual sequential → yearly. A final unknown-unit fact needs the original occurrence protection required by 5.9; do not merge it merely because numbers match.
6. **Period and movement:** establish the forecast's real target window or explicitly stated permitted horizon using its subject's verified calendar. Do not invent a year, a closing date or a long-term period. “Going forward” is `undefined`, not automatically long-term. Unresolved required period fails; source-unstated movement is `unknown`, not computed from outcomes or history (3.36–3.43, 4.4).
7. **Assumptions, measurement definitions and causes differ:** a stated forecast assumption belongs in `conditions` (4.8); wording that defines the measurement must be preserved under 3.24–3.26; causes must not be turned into assumptions (3.53). A sentence may contain more than one kind. Selecting it as supporting evidence does not authorize storing the entire sentence as a condition. An explicitly forecast numeric assumption can be its own eligible claim as well as supporting another claim; do not automatically duplicate every number in an assumption.
8. **Comparisons and other applicable fields:** preserve the source's permitted explicit comparison/baseline and revision, without substituting an actual or an older forecast for the current value (3.49–3.52, 4.6). Follow the current guidance schema, not legacy-only fields such as an extraction “derivation” category. Numberless values follow 4.7; do not hide a numeric value in `value_text`.
9. **Evidence:** exact, immutable source/version and occurrences; one continuous source quote must contain all words needed to establish the claim, including applicable conditions/negations and attribution (1.24, 8.8). A field already proved within that quote does not require a redundant separate Locate result. Conversely, a nonempty span, correct substring or high probability does not prove meaning, completeness or applicability. Do not assert that far-condition handling or cross-block source construction has been implemented merely because the rule does not forbid a multi-speaker quote.
10. **Open representations stay explicit:** centre ± tolerance, range-relative wording, blanket changes with uncertain prior scope, and other unresolved cases remain in the inventory with their evidence and reason. Current owner leanings are not approved rule changes. Do not force a shape or silently drop a difficult item to improve a score.
11. **Outcomes:** report complete correct proposals, wrong accepted proposals, missing/partial claims, extra claims, explicit holds and technical failures separately. A held eligible claim is incomplete extraction. Unknown fields allowed by the rules are different from failed extraction. Semantic ambiguity in the reference remains unadjudicated; never manufacture a convenient expected answer.
12. **Test boundary:** source-faithful proposals are the extraction trial's output; this is not authorization for Driver creation, identity matching, merging, graph writes or trading. Store exact requests, probabilities and evidence. Grade against independently read source claims, then freeze and test unseen calls; component success is not a complete-record or corpus-wide pass.

**Raw wording versus final naming:** an exact copied phrase containing a fiscal period is not, for that reason alone, failed extraction. Grade whether it identifies the correct underlying measure, then separately whether growth/change meaning and qualifiers are preserved. Final name cleanup/matching remains later. Conversely, `Q4 implied` and `average` do not identify the underlying measure. A stated comparison baseline may be valid without a comparison number (3.49); do not require a number the source never states.

## Correction to the original diagnostic

`main_path_assemble.py` required separate `measure`, `value` and `context` Locate outputs for every candidate and treated broad qualification candidates as incomplete record types. Those checks can expose real missing evidence, but their raw counts are not counts of missing facts. In particular, an existing exact numeric occurrence or a field already supported by the assembled quote does not need a second copy. The original 0-complete result remains the recorded result of that configuration; do not silently rescore it as a new end-to-end run. Reconcile actual outputs against this contract before selecting further fixes.
