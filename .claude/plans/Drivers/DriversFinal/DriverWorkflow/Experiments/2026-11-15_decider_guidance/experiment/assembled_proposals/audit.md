## Correct two over-strict checks

1. **Raw copied words are not a final Driver name.** `fiscal 2026 net interest expense` identifies a real measure, even though the period must be removed before final naming. Likewise, `adjusted diluted EPS` may identify the underlying measure for either a level or a growth forecast. The proposal must separately preserve growth/change and qualifying meaning. Rules 1.14, 2.17–2.18 and 4.6 require this distinction; they do not require one Locate span to be a finished canonical name.
2. **A comparison need not contain a comparison amount.** Rules 3.49/3.52 allow a stated baseline with empty comparison numbers. A positive baseline finding followed by no number is not automatically failed extraction. A number actually present but missed still is.

This does not rescue `Q4 implied` or `average` as measure names: neither identifies what is measured. The open reference/worded-forecast classes remain open. No original model response or baseline score is overwritten.
